About me

I used to think “About Me” pages were just a chore. But let's be honest: it's nice to know the person behind the work, especially in a digital age where a screen usually sits between us. So I'll keep this straightforward, and a little fun.

My background

I grew up watching how businesses run, and went into accountancy first. In my third year I found a genuine passion for marketing, took extra classes, and realised that stories move people far more than raw numbers ever will.

After graduating I worked at a leading UK agency, collaborating with brands including Oculus, Foot Locker, Diageo, Superdrug and AJ Bell. I then set up on my own, building a diverse client base across industries.

My education

I studied accountancy, which gave me a grounding in financial principles and how businesses actually operate. That knowledge still pays off in marketing every day: it's what lets me analyse data, assess ROI and build strategies that are financially grounded, not just creative for creativity's sake.

My approach

I'm a digital marketer aiming to rank among the UK's best. My approach is simple: review the performance, make quick, informed adjustments, and keep improving.

I care about the working relationship as much as the results, and I do my best work with friendly, ambitious people who'd rather keep things human than formal. I work across health & wellness, alcohol-free, gaming, finance, retail, e-commerce and SaaS. London-based, working worldwide.

That's enough about me. Let's talk about you.
